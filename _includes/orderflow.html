Self-exciting order flow

A limit order book, simulated live in your browser

Six kinds of order (market, limit and cancel, on each side of the book) arrive as a multivariate Hawkes process. Every order briefly raises the arrival rate of the others, so trading comes in bursts rather than at a steady pace.

λi(t) = μi + Σj ∫0t αij e−β(t−s) dNj(s)

The branching ratio is the average number of orders each order sets off (the spectral radius of α/β). Push it towards 1 and the bursts start feeding on themselves.
